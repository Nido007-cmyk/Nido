# BOAR first real-device baseline — 2026-09-24

Run `eval-2026-09-24T03-12-53-754Z`, eval set v1 (17 queries), 5 configurations, 85 answers, 66 minutes.
No code changes were made for or during this run.

## Setup

- Device: Xiaomi 2311DRK48G, MediaTek MT6897 (Dimensity 8300), 11.6 GB RAM, Android 16, charging.
- Every config: `succinct` system prompt, 512 max tokens, temperature 0.7, no conversation history.
- Battery temperature rose from 39 °C to 43 °C during the run, so later configs may have been
  thermally throttled. As a reference, Qwen 1.5B ran at 16–20 tok/s in a cooler test earlier and
  11–12 tok/s here.

- **Waiting for the first token is the main cost.** With retrieval on (6 chunks), TTFT is 5–7×
  higher than without: Phi 7–9s vs about 45s, Qwen 7B 11–16s vs about 70s, Qwen 1.5B 2.6s vs
  about 13s. Prompts aren't token-counted, but that implies roughly 20 tok/s prompt processing for
  Phi, 12 for Qwen 7B and 70 for Qwen 1.5B (estimates).
- **Evaluation and orchestration overhead is small.** Total time minus load, TTFT and generation is
  1–2s per query with retrieval (query embedding, search, prompt assembly) and about 0s without.
- **Memory is far under the 12 GB budget** for every model that ran.
- **The timeout hits the important categories.** The 120s per-step safety limit cut off 9 answers,
  all synthesis, comparison and reasoning questions from Phi and Qwen 7B.

## 2. Model-by-model

- **Qwen 1.5B:** fast and correct on simple, explanation and grounded questions. On grounded
  questions it mostly copies the retrieved text. It makes reasoning and factual slips:
  - says the French and Industrial Revolutions "occurred in different eras", but they overlapped
  - says the Amazon "regulates temperature by producing oxygen"
  - calculates 5 GB left and says a 9 GB model "would fit"
  - rarely cites sources
- **Phi-3.5:** the most complete and structured comparison and synthesis answers; best on
  `comparison-2` and `synthesis-2`, and the only model that correctly said a 9 GB model won't fit.
  - Invented sources: "National Geographic, WWF, NASA" references, and a fake source
    "[2] Misuse and overuse of antibiotics".
  - Wrong arithmetic: 7.5 GB left (the right figure is 8.3 GB), and a "minimum disk footprint = 8B"
    answer.
  - Cut off: timed out 4 times.
- **Qwen 7B:** accurate, and the best antibiotics answer (with beta-lactam and erythromycin
  examples).
  - Too slow for this timeout: at 2.7 tok/s it timed out 5 times, often mid-sentence
    ("threatens more than", "taking approximately 80 years").
  - Arithmetic: 9.5 GB left, so "would fit"; and "8 bytes per token" for the MoE disk footprint.
  - Placeholder citation: wrote a literal "[n]".
- **Instella:** didn't produce any answers, so it can't be compared (see section 7).

## 3. Answer comparison (highlights)

Full side-by-side answers: `eval-2026-09-24T03-12-53-754Z.answers.md`.

- **Factual and greeting:** all correct. Greetings were short with no rambling, including Qwen.
- **Explanation (vaccines, photosynthesis):** all correct. Qwen 1.5B's photosynthesis answer leaves
  out oxygen.
- **Comparison and synthesis:** best answers from Phi, then Qwen 7B (often truncated), then
  Qwen 1.5B (shallower and extractive, with the errors above).
- **Reasoning:**
  - `reasoning-1` (train time): correct from all three fixed configs; adaptive (Qwen 1.5B, same
    model and prompt) said **7:15 pm**, which is sampling variance.
  - `reasoning-2` (RAM budget): **every model got the arithmetic wrong.**
  - `reasoning-3` (MoE footprint): right from Qwen 1.5B and adaptive→Phi (about 100 GB); wrong from
    Phi-with-template and Qwen 7B.
- **Grounded (Rome, black hole):** all correct and well supported by the retrieved article.
- **No-KB (Napoleon):** all correct from the models' own knowledge. Phi cited an unrelated "[5]",
  and adaptive's Qwen answer contains stray Chinese characters ("the疆场") plus the error "legacy
  includes the French Revolution".
- **Citation formatting is unreliable across all models:** invented reference lists, "[n]", an
  out-of-range "[7]", and trailing "Sources: [1], [2], [3]".

## 4. Retrieval

- **The expected article was found 10/10 times, always ranked #1 or #2.** Retrieval was identical
  across configs.
- **Every retrieving query returned exactly 6 chunks.** The 0.45 floor never excluded anything, and
  2–5 of each 6 are unrelated: Tim Peto, CRISPR and "Complementarity-determining region" for
  vaccines; Mold and "Balloon modelling" for black holes; six railway articles, including
  "4:17 am", for the train question.
  - Irrelevant chunks were mostly ignored, though Phi pulled the "Complementarity-determining
    region" chunk into its immune-system answer.
  - The capital-of-Australia question retrieved six Australian articles, none about Canberra.
- **Two problems with the eval set, found while analyzing:**
  - `no-kb-2` does have a relevant article ("Antimicrobial resistance"). The earlier check only
    searched titles for "antibiotic".
  - The default knowledge base includes **five hand-written articles** (`APP_TOPIC_DOCS`
    in `src/rag/seedCorpus.ts`, installed on every phone): "Mixture-of-Experts models
    and phone RAM", "Budgeting RAM for on-device LLM inference", "mmap-based weight
    streaming vs. full RAM loading", "BM25 lexical search vs. cosine similarity over
    embeddings", "GrapheneOS and Google Play Services". They match the original demo
    questions, and `reasoning-3` retrieved two of them, so that answer partly comes from
    a document written for it. (An earlier version of this report said they were
    leftovers only on this phone; that was wrong.)

## 5. Prompt and template

Every fixed-model config used its own chat template on all 17 queries: Phi's `<|system|>`, Qwen's
ChatML. Adaptive routing used a template only for Qwen 1.5B; its 3 Phi answers used the plain
prompt, as designed for live chat. So none of the fixed-model results mix prompt formats.

## 6. Adaptive routing (`balanced` preset)

- **Model choice:** 14/17 went to Qwen 1.5B, 3/17 to Phi. **Qwen 7B was never eligible**, because
  `balanced` has no reasoning role, so compare/research tasks fell back to the general role (Phi).
- **Classification gaps:**
  - `comparison-2` ("Contrast…") and `synthesis-2`/`synthesis-3` were classified as `chat` and
    routed to the smallest model, though the answers were acceptable.
  - `reasoning-1` was classified as `chat`, so it retrieved six irrelevant railway articles and got
    the time wrong.
  - `reasoning-2` was classified as `calculate`, which correctly skipped retrieval, but the answer
    was still wrong.
  - `comparison-1`, `synthesis-1` and `reasoning-3` were routed to Phi, which fits those tasks.
- **Other decisions:** greetings got the 128-token cap and skipped retrieval; verification never
  ran (not applicable under this preset); there were no switches within a plan.
- **Switching cost:** 6 cross-message switches, because the query order alternates task types,
  adding 4.5–8.9s of load time each (about 7% of adaptive's total time).

## 7. Failures

- **Instella failed to load on all 17 attempts,** about 1.6s each, and the run carried on cleanly.
  The app's error message blames RAM, but 8.7 GB was free and it needs about 7 GB. The real cause is
  almost certainly that this llama.rn build (0.13.0-rc.4) doesn't support the `instella-moe`
  architecture:
  - none of the seven Android native libraries contain an `instella` string (they do contain
    `deepseek2` and `qwen2moe`)
  - the load fails at the file-parsing stage, within about 130 ms
  - llama.cpp didn't log an explicit reason, so this isn't directly proven
- **No crashes or out-of-memory errors.**
- **Nine answers were truncated by the timeout.** They're recorded as `success` with
  `timedOut: true`: Phi `synthesis-1/2/3`, `no-kb-2`; Qwen 7B `comparison-2`, `synthesis-1/2/3`,
  `reasoning-3`.
