# NIDO Economic Layer — Architecture

**Status:** RESEARCH / DESIGN ONLY. No implementation.
No token, no blockchain integration, no changes to the current protocol
(`AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`, `CAPABILITY_MODEL.md` intact), no Android changes.

**Relationship to principles:** this document is subordinate to
`../NIDO_PRINCIPLES.md`. In case of conflict, the principles prevail.
Key references: §4 (separation of responsibilities), §8
(long horizon, zero-trust, minimum disclosure, crypto agility).

**Decision classification.** Each substantive decision carries exactly one
of these labels:

- **[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** — not negotiable without explicit user review.
- **[PROVISIONAL]** — current design decision, revisable with evidence.
- **[EXPERIMENTAL]** — hypothesis to validate, not to be assumed by any
  dependent design.
- **[OPEN QUESTIONS]** — undecided; question recorded for future research.

---

## 1. What problem this research line solves

A future network of personal NIDO agents could exchange work,
resources, and capabilities among themselves: compute, storage, specialized
processing, services. If that exchange happens, it must happen under the
same authority rules as everything else in NIDO.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** The economy is an **optional** layer. NIDO works
fully without a token, without blockchain, without a bank account, without
a central server, and offline where the task allows it. No basic capability,
no protocol flow, and no policy decision may depend
on the existence of the economic layer. A NIDO with the economy disabled
is a complete NIDO, not a degraded version.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** USER = AUTHORITY applies to money with the same
force as to data. The model may **propose** a transaction. The
**Policy Engine determines** whether it is permitted. **The user remains
the authority.** No economic flow may move value without a traceable
policy decision or an explicit user confirmation, as applicable.

## 2. Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this document |
|---|---|
| **Capability (economic primitive)** | A versioned capability with schema (`CAPABILITY_MODEL.md`) plus an economic descriptor. It is the unit of what gets exchanged. |
| **Offer** | DATA: a NIDO declares it can execute a capability under certain economic terms. Not authority, not commitment. |
| **Quote** | DATA: concrete price and conditions for a specific request, with TTL. A quote authorizes nothing. |
| **Authorization** | Decision by the local Policy Engine (or the user) to accept terms. This does have consequences. |
| **Commitment** | Registered mutual acceptance: the executor reserves resources, the requester reserves budget. Still moves no value. |
| **Settlement** | The **only** point where an economic side effect appears: value changes hands. At-most-once per `payment_id`. |
| **Receipt** | Local record, signed by the NIDO, of economic events and decisions. Never contains chain-of-thought. |
| **Rail** | Value movement mechanism (fiat, stablecoin, offline credits, …). Interchangeable. |
| **SettlementAdapter** | Abstract role connecting the economic layer to a concrete rail, without the protocol depending on the rail. |
| **Budget vault** | Operating budget separate from the user's main funds (see `ECONOMIC_POLICY_MODEL.md`). |
| **Verification** | Checking that the work was done before settling. Several modes; none universal (see `SETTLEMENT_ABSTRACTION.md`). |
| **Payment ID** | Unique identifier (128 bits, generated by the payer) that makes settlement idempotent. |

## 3. CAPABILITY IS THE ECONOMIC PRIMITIVE

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** The fundamental asset of the NIDO economy **is not
a token**. It is a **capability that can be requested, quoted,
negotiated, authorized, executed, verified, and settled**.

Design consequences:

1. Every economic exchange is expressed as an invocation of a
   versioned capability with schema (`name/version`), never as an abstract
   transfer of "value".
2. The price lives in the capability's **economic descriptor**, not in
   the protocol. The protocol (`TASK_REQUEST` / `TASK_RESULT`) does not know
   what money is.
3. A NIDO does not "pay to pay": it pays for the **verifiable execution**
   of a concrete capability, under quoted and authorized terms.
4. This makes the economy inherit the capability model's properties for
   free: versioning, minimum disclosure, sensitivity, human
   approval, attenuated delegation, idempotency by `task_id`.

**[PROVISIONAL]** Purely economic capabilities (e.g.
`payment.settle/v1` as an internal capability of the local NIDO, never
exposed to peers) are modeled with the same capability format, but with
`sensitivity: high`, `human_approval: always` by default and
`delegation_permitted: false`. Exposing settlement to a remote peer would be
confusing the executor with the custodian: the one who executes work is not
the one who moves the user's money.

## 4. Economic descriptor of a capability

A future capability may express the following economic fields in structured
form. The list is closed per descriptor version; adding fields is a new
version, never a silent extension.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `cost` | `{amount, unit}`. `amount` is an exact decimal number as a **string** (never binary float: see rounding attack in `ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md`). `unit` is a unit/currency identifier opaque to the protocol. |
| `currency_unit` | Explicit alias of `unit` when the unit is monetary. The protocol does not validate currencies; the rail's SettlementAdapter does. |
| `estimated_duration` | `{min_ms, max_ms}` or `unknown`. Informational commitment, not an SLA, unless `conditions` says otherwise. |
| `resource_requirements` | What the **executor** needs: `{compute_class, memory_mb_max, storage_mb_max, network_required: bool}`. Declarative and auditable. |
| `conditions` | Closed list of structured conditions (time window, requester retry limit, etc.). Free text prohibited as a binding condition. |
| `privacy_requirements` | What requester data execution needs and with what retention (see `ECONOMIC_PRIVACY.md`). |
| `refundability` | `full` / `partial:{terms}` / `none`, plus in which states it applies (see §5). `none` must be explicit, never the silent default. |
| `verification_method` | One or more of the modes in `SETTLEMENT_ABSTRACTION.md` §3, declared by the offeror and **chosen by the payer's policy**. |
| `quote_ttl_ms` | How long a quote based on this descriptor is valid. |

DATA example (not authority, not code):

```json
{
  "capability": "compute.batch.render/v1",
  "economic": {
    "cost": { "amount": "0.40", "unit": "USD" },
    "estimated_duration": { "min_ms": 60000, "max_ms": 600000 },
    "resource_requirements": { "compute_class": "gpu", "memory_mb_max": 4096, "network_required": false },
    "conditions": ["input_size_mb_max: 500", "output_retention_hours: 24"],
    "privacy_requirements": { "input_data_retention": "until_result_delivered", "logging": "no_input_content" },
    "refundability": "partial:{terms: pro_rata_before_50pct}",
    "verification_method": ["deterministic_verification", "buyer_confirmation"],
    "quote_ttl_ms": 300000
  }
}
```

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** An offer is **DATA**. No economic field is
authoritative by itself: neither the price, nor `refundability`, nor
`verification_method` binds anyone. The Policy Engine of the NIDO receiving
the offer decides what is acceptable; the payer's policy decides whether to
pay. A malicious peer can lie in every field: the design must
remain safe when the offer lies (see `ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md`:
quote substitution, bait-and-switch).

**[PROVISIONAL]** The economic descriptor travels as part of an extended
`CAPABILITY_RESPONSE` or a future quote message, but **not** in the public
discovery announcement: announcing prices publicly leaks economic
information (see `ECONOMIC_PRIVACY.md`). Price only
upon authenticated request and within policy.

## 5. Economic state machine

Deterministic flow. These are the only states that exist; there are no
implicit transitions.

```
DISCOVER → REQUEST_QUOTE → QUOTE ⇄ COUNTER → ACCEPT_QUOTE → AUTHORIZE
                                                          → COMMIT → EXECUTE
                                                          → VERIFY → SETTLE → RECEIPT
```

Alternative paths (from the indicated state):

- From `QUOTE` / `COUNTER`: `DECLINE` (terminal, no effects).
- From any state before `COMMIT`: `EXPIRE` (by `quote_ttl_ms` or
  `expires_at`; terminal, no effects, releases reservations if any existed).
- From `ACCEPT_QUOTE` / `AUTHORIZE` / `COMMIT`: `CANCEL` (terminal; if
  `COMMIT` already happened, the refund branch applies per `refundability`).
- From `EXECUTE` / `VERIFY`: `FAIL` (terminal; triggers `REFUND` if
  `refundability != none`).
- From `SETTLE`: `REFUND` (inverse economic side effect, idempotent).
- From `VERIFY` (disagreement) or `SETTLE` (claim): `DISPUTE`
  (freezes; resolved only by explicit human arbitration; terminal after
  resolution toward `SETTLE`, `REFUND`, or `CANCEL`).

### 5.1 When exactly a side effect appears

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** There are two classes of side effect and they
appear at distinct, unambiguously defined points:

1. **Execution side effect** (work/resources consumed): appears
   **exactly upon entering `EXECUTE`**, and only if `COMMIT` was traversed
   beforehand. Nothing before `COMMIT` consumes the executor's resources
   beyond the trivial cost of answering messages. `CANCEL` before `COMMIT`
   owes nothing to anyone.

2. **Economic side effect** (value changes hands): appears
   **exactly upon entering `SETTLE`**, once per `payment_id`,
   and only if `VERIFY` concluded per the verification mode
   the **payer's policy** accepted. No other state moves value.
   `AUTHORIZE` and `COMMIT` only create **reservations** (reduction of
   available budget, without transfer). Reservations are released on
   `EXPIRE`, `DECLINE`, `CANCEL` (pre-`COMMIT`), and `FAIL`/`REFUND`.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** A retry **never** produces a double payment. Settlement
is idempotent by `payment_id` (128 bits, generated by the
payer, unique per payment intent):

- The settlement journal is indexed by `payment_id`. The first transition
  to `SETTLE` executes the transfer and saves the receipt.
- Any later retry with the same `payment_id` returns the
  saved receipt **without** touching the rail. There is no "re-settling":
  the `SETTLE` state is terminal for that `payment_id`.
- `REFUND` is a separate side effect, idempotent by `refund_id`,
  allowed only from `SETTLED`. A refund does not "un-do" the payment in
  the journal: it offsets it with a new entry.
- This mirrors at the economic level the protocol's `task_id` discipline
  (INV-4 RETRY_SAFETY): at-most-once on side effects, at-least-once on
  receipt delivery.

**[PROVISIONAL]** `payment_id` and `task_id` are distinct identifiers
with distinct purposes: `task_id` for **execution** idempotency,
`payment_id` for **settlement** idempotency. A task may have zero or
one settlement; a settlement references exactly one task
(the charge's origin), never an open chain.

### 5.2 Transition table (normative summary)

| From | Event | To | Side effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DISCOVER` | quote request | `REQUEST_QUOTE` | none |
| `REQUEST_QUOTE` | quote received and valid | `QUOTE` | none |
| `QUOTE` | counter-offer | `COUNTER` | none |
| `COUNTER` | new quote / acceptance | `QUOTE` / `ACCEPT_QUOTE` | none |
| `QUOTE` | rejection | `DECLINE` | none (terminal) |
| `QUOTE`/`COUNTER`/`REQUEST_QUOTE` | timeout | `EXPIRE` | none (terminal) |
| `ACCEPT_QUOTE` | policy/user decision | `AUTHORIZE` | budget reservation (local) |
| `AUTHORIZE` | mutual commitment | `COMMIT` | resource reservation (executor) |
| `AUTHORIZE`/`COMMIT` | cancellation | `CANCEL` | releases reservations; if post-`COMMIT`, evaluates `refundability` |
| `COMMIT` | work start | `EXECUTE` | **execution side effect** |
| `EXECUTE` | work complete / failed | `VERIFY` / `FAIL` | no new side effect / triggers refund |
| `VERIFY` | verification OK | `SETTLE` | **economic side effect (single)** |
| `VERIFY` | disagreement | `DISPUTE` | freezes |
| `SETTLE` | recorded | `RECEIPT` | none (record) |
| `SETTLED` | refund authorized | `REFUND` | inverse economic side effect (idempotent) |
| `DISPUTE` | human arbitration | `SETTLE` / `REFUND` / `CANCEL` | per resolution |

**[PROVISIONAL]** `DISPUTE` always requires a human in the loop. No
automatic dispute arbitration is designed: an algorithm deciding money
disputes is an authority nobody granted.

## 6. Agents can earn: the NIDO as provider

**[PROVISIONAL]** A user may allow their NIDO to **offer**
capabilities to others (compute, storage, specialized
processing, business services, automated services). This is
explicit opt-in, off by default, and is configured with:

- **what** it may offer: closed list of `capability/version`.
- **to whom**: allowed peers (specific identities, or relationship
  classes: family, known business). Default: nobody.
- **when**: time windows, and never while the device is in
  interactive use unless explicitly authorized.
- **limits**: per task, per peer, per day (compute time, bytes,
  number of executions).
- **price/conditions**: economic descriptor per offered capability.
- **max resources**: hard cap on CPU/memory/storage/battery
  an offered task may consume. **[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** A peer's task
  can never exceed the configured cap, even if the quote
  "allows" it.
- **data that may never be used**: hard exclusion list (e.g.
  message contents, calendar, location, keys). **[FROZEN
  PRINCIPLES]** Excluded data never enters the offered task's execution
  environment by any path: not as input, not as model context,
  not as a log side-channel.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** The offered task executes with the **buyer's
authority** (confused deputy, `CAPABILITY_MODEL.md` §4), never with the
device owner's. The provider NIDO is a sandboxed executor of the
sold capability, not an agent with the user's privileges.

**[EXPERIMENTAL]** Dynamic pricing (demand-based adjustment) — not designed;
a price that changes on its own is a manipulation surface (see redteam:
bait-and-switch).

## 7. Group tasks: buying from multiple providers

Flow: `plan → quote multiple providers → choose → authorize budgets →
execute graph → verify nodes → settle nodes`.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** The Policy Engine validates **each spend and each
node** separately. Accepting a complete task graph **never** grants unlimited
budget or blanket authorization:

1. Each graph node carries its own quote and its own `payment_id`.
2. The total authorized budget is the **explicit sum** of the approved
   nodes, plus a contingency reserve that is **also explicit**
   (percentage or absolute cap set by policy, default 0).
3. A node exceeding its quote cannot "borrow" from another node's budget.
   A node's overrun → new `REQUEST_QUOTE` →
   new policy decision.
4. `SETTLE` per node: a verified node settles even if another fails. There
   is no "all or nothing" unless the user's policy explicitly requires it
   for that graph (and even then, each settlement remains idempotent
   by its `payment_id`).

**[PROVISIONAL]** The planner (model) proposes the graph; the Policy
Engine authorizes it node by node. The model never rewrites quotes or
merges budgets: that would be authority disguised as optimization.

## 8. Crypto agility: identity decoupled from money

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** The NIDO identity (long-term key,
device certificates, rotation chain) **is not a wallet** and is not
coupled to any rail, payment provider, blockchain, currency, or
settlement mechanism. A person must be able to change wallet,
provider, blockchain, currency, and rail **without changing their NIDO
identity** and without re-pairing contacts.

Conceptual design:

- Payment credentials live in a separate **settlement profile**,
  custodied locally (same protection level as identity keys, but different
  keys, independent rotation).
- The NIDO identity signs **spend authorizations** (which `payment_id`,
  how much, to whom, under which policy); the settlement profile executes
  the movement on the rail. Losing/rotating the profile does not invalidate
  the identity, and compromising the profile does not hand over the identity.
- Peers never need the settlement profile's key: they only see the
  authorization signed by the identity and the rail's settlement proof.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** Rotating payment credentials never requires
identity rotation, and identity rotation never automatically exposes
payment credentials to the new device: the user
re-authorizes them explicitly.

## 9. What this design refuses to do (non-goals)

- Does not define tokenomics, supply, price, or incentives of any token.
- Does not select a blockchain, currency, or rail.
- Does not add messages to the current protocol or change `TASK_REQUEST`.
- Does not implement anything on Android or in `src/`.
- Does not promise prevention of impossible offline double-spend (see
  `ECONOMIC_POLICY_MODEL.md` §6 and `ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md`).
- Does not design automatic dispute arbitration.
- Does not turn economic metrics (volume, balance) into reputation,
  trust, or authority (see `ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md` §18).

## 10. New ideas: offline-first economic goods

**[EXPERIMENTAL]** Beyond compute-for-hire, the goods that fit NIDO's
offline-first nature best are *data goods the buyer can verify without
trusting the seller*. Three directions, all research-only:

### 10.1 Knowledge pack exchange

NIDO knowledge packs (`KNOWLEDGE_PACKS.md`) are content-addressed
(SHA-256 per pack, per-chunk hashes) and signed by the curator. A NIDO
could offer a curated pack (a medical reference pack, an offline
trail-guide pack, a specialized corpus) to another NIDO over
P2P/Bluetooth/Wi-Fi Direct, fully offline.

Why this fits: verification is **deterministic** (mode 1 of
`SETTLEMENT_ABSTRACTION.md` §4) — the buyer recomputes hashes before
`SETTLE`. There is no "fake completion" ambiguity: either the bytes match
the manifest or they don't. The economic descriptor gains goods-specific
fields: `pack_id`, `pack_hash`, `chunk_count`, `license_id`.

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** No pack is offered, quoted, or settled without its
license metadata traveling with it, and the buyer's Policy Engine
evaluates license compatibility before `AUTHORIZE`. NIDO never
facilitates license violation as a feature: a pack derived from CC BY-SA
sources (e.g. Wikipedia) carries share-alike obligations, and the buyer is
informed of them wherever consent is collected. License laundering
(stripping attribution or share-alike terms) is an attack — see
`ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md` §19.

**[PROVISIONAL]** Chunked transfer with resume: packs are hundreds of MB;
transfer is chunked per content hash, each chunk acknowledged, resumable
across sessions and transports. The quote prices the pack, not the chunk;
partial delivery settles pro-rata per `refundability`.

Trade-offs: bandwidth (Bluetooth-class links make large packs
impractical; Wi-Fi Direct/LAN is the realistic transport); curation trust
(the hash proves integrity, not quality — quality is reputation,
`ECONOMIC_REDTEAM.md` §18); freshness (packs version — who pays for
updates, full re-buy vs. delta?).

**[OPEN QUESTIONS]** Can a pack's *usefulness* be sampled before purchase
without leaking the whole pack (e.g. a verifiable random chunk preview)?
How are pack updates priced?

### 10.2 Model weight sharing

A NIDO that already downloaded a GGUF could serve it to a peer over local
transport. Integrity is trivially verifiable (SHA-256 checksums in
`MODELS.md`).

**[FROZEN PRINCIPLES]** Redistribution license gate: model licenses (LFM
Open License, Gemma Terms) may prohibit redistribution. Until counsel
clears a specific model for peer redistribution, offering model weights is
**not designed**. This gate is legal, not technical, and no technical
cleverness bypasses it.

Trade-offs: multi-GB transfers need Wi-Fi Direct/LAN-class transport, not
Bluetooth; the seeder spends battery and bandwidth; version skew (exactly
which model revision is served?).

Classification: **[OPEN QUESTIONS]** — effectively blocked on the legal
gate above.

### 10.3 Deferred netting

For repeated bilateral trade, settling every micro-payment on an external
rail is friction without benefit. See `SETTLEMENT_ABSTRACTION.md` §6:
only the net amount settles.

## 11. Final principle

**NIDO MAY NEGOTIATE VALUE.
NIDO MAY EXCHANGE VALUE.
NIDO MAY EARN VALUE.**

**BUT NIDO NEVER OWNS THE USER'S AUTHORITY.**

**THE MODEL MAY PROPOSE A TRANSACTION.
THE POLICY ENGINE DETERMINES WHETHER IT IS PERMITTED.
THE USER REMAINS THE AUTHORITY.**

## Open questions (this document)

1. Should the economic descriptor travel inside `CAPABILITY_RESPONSE` or
   in a separate quote message with its own lifecycle?
2. What granularity of `compute_class` is sufficient without leaking the
   device's real hardware (privacy vs. quote utility)?
3. How is `refundability: partial` expressed in structured, verifiable
   form without a Turing-complete condition language?
4. Should there be a global "value in flight" limit (unsettled
   reservations) in addition to per-task/day limits?
5. Can a knowledge pack's usefulness be sampled pre-purchase without
   leaking the pack (verifiable random chunk preview)?
6. How are knowledge pack updates priced — full re-buy or delta?
