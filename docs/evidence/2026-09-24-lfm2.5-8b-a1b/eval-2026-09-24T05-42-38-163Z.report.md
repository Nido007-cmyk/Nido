Run `eval-2026-09-24T05-42-38-163Z`, eval set v1 (17 queries), one config, 15 minutes.
Same phone as the baseline (Xiaomi 2311DRK48G, Dimensity 8300, 11.6 GB RAM); battery
34.5 °C at start, 42.7 °C at the end.

- About 85% of each output was reasoning (377–2,711 characters of thinking vs 34–550
  characters of answer).
- **4 of 17 produced no answer**: the reasoning used the whole 512-token budget
  (`comparison-2`, `synthesis-3`, `reasoning-3`, `grounded-1`). `synthesis-2`'s answer
  was cut off mid-sentence.
- TTFT with retrieval is about 25s; without retrieval (greeting, RAM question) 7–8s.

## Answers (where one arrived)

- Correct: capital of Australia (plus when Canberra became capital), Darwin (and
  Wallace), vaccines, photosynthesis, black hole, antibiotics and resistance, train time
  (6:15 pm).
- **First model to get the RAM question right**: "About 8.3 GB remains, and a 9 GB model
  would not fit."
- Comparison and synthesis answers that finished were accurate and concise, with
  citations.
- Errors: Napoleon "established the French First Republic" (wrong); a placeholder
  "[n]" citation; the photosynthesis answer opens with "Hi!".

## Takeaway

Fastest generation of anything tested, and the best reasoning when it finishes, but
with 512 tokens of budget the thinking often leaves no room for the answer. The next
experiments are a larger token budget for reasoning models, and LFM2-8B-A1B (same
architecture, no thinking) for comparison.
