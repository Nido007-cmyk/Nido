Storing water before an emergency is different from making water safe during one (that is in NIDO Essentials' safe water guide). This is the supply: clean water, in safe containers, ready for drinking, cooking and washing.

## How much water to store
- **1 gallon per person per day (about 3.8 liters)**, for drinking and basic hygiene.
- Store enough for **at least 3 days** and, if you can, for **2 weeks**.
- Example: a family of 4 needs about 12 gallons (45 liters) for 3 days and 56 gallons (about 212 liters) for 2 weeks.
- Store more for pregnant women, people who are sick, pets, or if you live in a hot climate.
- Unopened, commercially bottled water is the safest and most reliable supply. Observe its expiration date.

## Safe containers
- Best: FDA-approved food-grade water storage containers, with a top that closes tightly.
- If you can't get those, choose a container with a tight-fitting top, made of durable, unbreakable material (not glass), with a narrow neck or opening if possible so you can pour from it.
- **Never** use containers that previously held toxic chemicals, such as bleach or pesticides.

## Clean and sanitize the container before filling it
1. Wash it with soap and rinse completely with water.
2. Sanitize it with a solution of **1 teaspoon of unscented liquid household chlorine bleach** (5%–9% sodium hypochlorite) in **1 quart of water (4 cups)**.
3. Cover the container tightly and shake it well, so the solution touches all inside surfaces.
4. Wait **at least 30 seconds**, then pour the solution out.
5. Let the empty container air-dry.
6. Fill it with clean water and cover it with a tight lid.

## Storing it
- Label each container "drinking water" and include the storage date.
- Keep it in a cool place, **50°F–70°F (10–21°C)**, away from direct sunlight.
- Keep it away from toxic substances, such as gasoline or pesticides.
- **Replace water you filled yourself every 6 months.** Put the date on your calendar and rotate: use the old water for plants or cleaning and refill.

## Using it
- Take water out with a clean scoop or other clean device, a clean one each time.
- Do not touch the water or the insides of the container with your hands, and do not scoop water out with your hands.
- If your stored water did not come from a safe source, treat it before drinking, as the NIDO Essentials safe water guide explains (boiling is the safest method).
