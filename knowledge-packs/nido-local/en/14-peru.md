In Peru, the checked emergency numbers are: 105 (police), 116 (fire department), 106 (medical emergencies). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Peru | medical emergency number in Peru -->
- **105**: police
- **116**: fire department
- **106**: medical emergencies (Ministry of Health SAMU, free; EsSalud members also have 117)

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Ambulance: 106 or 117
The Ministry of Health's SAMU (106) is free; the official gazette El Peruano quotes it as "in any emergency, stay calm and call 106 right away". 117 is the EsSalud line for its insured members. For disasters, the authority is the National Civil Defense Institute (INDECI).
