In Uruguay, the single emergency number is 9-1-1: it is the number to call in any emergency. Use it only for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who warns of a disaster
Uruguay's National Emergency System (SINAE) coordinates the response to emergencies and disasters; its advice is: in any emergency, call 9-1-1.
