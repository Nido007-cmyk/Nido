In Honduras, the checked emergency numbers are: 911 (emergencies (single number)), 198 (fire department), 195 (ambulance), 113 (civil protection and disasters). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Honduras | medical emergency number in Honduras -->
- **911**: emergencies (single number)
- **198**: fire department
- **195**: ambulance
- **113**: civil protection and disasters

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Who warns of a disaster
The Permanent Contingency Commission (COPECO), Honduras's civil protection, answers 113.
