In the Dominican Republic, the emergency number is 9-1-1, from anywhere in the country (Decree 187-14).

## Who warns
The Emergency Operations Center (COE) issues the official alerts for rain, storms and hurricanes, province by province. They are announced in its bulletins, on radio and on television: without internet, keep a battery-powered radio.

## What each COE alert means
- **Green alert:** stay alert to the event. Check your emergency bag, water and medicines.
- **Yellow alert:** be ready to move to a safe place. Decide where you would go (a shelter, or the home of family or friends in a safe area) and have everything ready.
- **Red alert:** be ready to leave or already be in a safe place (a shelter or the home of friends and family).

## If a hurricane comes
- If you are ordered to evacuate, go immediately.
- If not, stay in the smallest interior room on the lowest floor, away from windows and glass doors.
- Go home only when the authorities say it is safe.
- Afterwards: do not cross flooded streets or damaged bridges, stay away from downed power lines and never run a generator inside the house or garage.

## Important
Alerts change by the hour: always follow the COE bulletins for your province.
