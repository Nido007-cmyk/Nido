In Spain, the single emergency number is 112 (free from landlines and mobiles, even with the phone locked): it is the number to call in any emergency. Use it only for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call

## ES-Alert: the warning on your phone
Spain's Civil Protection sends ES-Alert warnings to every mobile phone in the danger area, with no app and no registration. It arrives with a beep and vibration. Phones that are off or in airplane mode don't get it. If you are driving, stop somewhere safe before reading it. Read the message and do what it says; to ask for help, call 112.
