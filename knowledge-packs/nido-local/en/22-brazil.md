In Brazil, the checked emergency numbers are: 190 (police), 192 (ambulance), 193 (fire department). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Brazil | medical emergency number in Brazil -->
- **190**: police
- **192**: ambulance (SAMU)
- **193**: fire department

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Civil Defense alerts by text message
In Brazil, the Civil Defense (Defesa Civil) sends alerts by text message. To get them, text the postal code (CEP) of your area to 40199; you get a confirmation message and from then on you receive the warnings for that area.
