In Chile, the checked emergency numbers are: 133 (police), 131 (medical emergencies), 132 (fire department). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Chile | medical emergency number in Chile -->
- **133**: police
- **131**: medical emergencies
- **132**: fire department

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## The SAE alert on your phone
SENAPRED sends Emergency Alert System (SAE) alerts to the phones in the area at risk. They arrive as a pop-up message with a loud sound at full volume and vibration. What SENAPRED asks: act calmly and follow the instructions of the authorities and response teams. If the message tells you to evacuate, do it.
