In Nicaragua, the checked emergency numbers are: 118 (police), 115 (fire department), 128 (medical emergencies), 101 (tourist police). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Nicaragua | medical emergency number in Nicaragua -->
- **118**: police
- **115**: fire department
- **128**: medical emergencies
- **101**: tourist police
