In Colombia, the checked emergency numbers are: 123 (fire department), 123 (medical emergencies), 112 (police). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Colombia | medical emergency number in Colombia -->
- **123**: fire department
- **123**: medical emergencies
- **112**: police

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Who warns of a disaster
Colombia's National Disaster Risk Management Unit (UNGRD) coordinates disaster response.
