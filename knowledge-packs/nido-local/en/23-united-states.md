In the United States, the single emergency number is 911: it is the number to call in any emergency. Use it only for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Alerts on your phone (WEA)
Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA) are free messages sent directly to your cell phone to warn you about severe weather and other threats. There is nothing to sign up for: compatible phones already get them (ask your carrier). They come with a unique sound and vibration. Do not ignore them: the message is short, so look for more information on radio or TV.
