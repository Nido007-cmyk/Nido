In Costa Rica, the checked emergency numbers are: 911 (emergencies (single number)), 118 (fire department), 128 (medical emergencies). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Costa Rica | medical emergency number in Costa Rica -->
- **911**: emergencies (single number)
- **118**: fire department (or 911)
- **128**: medical emergencies (or 911)

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Who warns of a disaster
In Costa Rica, disaster emergencies are coordinated by the National Commission for Risk Prevention and Emergency Response (CNE), governed by the National Emergency and Risk Prevention Law (Law 8488).
