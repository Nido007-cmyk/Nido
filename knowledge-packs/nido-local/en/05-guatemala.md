In Guatemala, the checked emergency numbers are: 110 (police), 122 (fire department), 123 (municipal fire department), 125 (Red Cross), 119 (civil protection and disasters). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Guatemala | medical emergency number in Guatemala -->
- **110**: police
- **122**: fire department
- **123**: municipal fire department
- **125**: Red Cross
- **119**: civil protection and disasters (CONRED; also 1566)

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Who warns of a disaster
Guatemala's disaster agency, CONRED, answers 119 and 1566. Follow its notices on the radio if the power or internet goes down.
