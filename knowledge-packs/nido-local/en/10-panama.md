In Panama, the checked emergency numbers are: 104 (police), 911 (ambulance), 103 (fire department). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Panama | medical emergency number in Panama -->
- **104**: police
- **911**: ambulance
- **103**: fire department

Checked on 2026-10-28 against official sources; each number comes with its quote in this pack's facts table.

## Who warns of a disaster
Panama's National Civil Protection System (SINAPROC) issues prevention notices for rain and storms and sets the alert levels by province. If your province goes on alert, follow what SINAPROC says.
