In Argentina, the checked emergency numbers are: 911 (police), 100 (fire department), 107 (medical emergencies). They are for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who to call
<!-- questions: fire department number in Argentina | medical emergency number in Argentina -->
- **911**: police
- **100**: fire department
- **107**: medical emergencies
