In Ecuador, the single emergency number is 911: it is the number to call in any emergency. Use it only for real emergencies: if you can, say where you are first.

## Who warns of a disaster
Ecuador's National Risk Management Secretariat (SNGR) declares colour-coded alert levels; for example, in 2023 it raised the alert from yellow to orange because of El Niño. Follow its resolutions and notices.
