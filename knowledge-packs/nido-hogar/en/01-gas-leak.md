The gas that feeds your stove, boiler or water heater is invisible. When it escapes, a single spark can ignite it and cause an explosion. If you suspect a leak, the answer is always the same: get out and call from outside.

## How to spot a leak
- **A rotten-egg or sulfur smell.** Natural gas has no smell of its own: a substance (mercaptan) with that strong odor is added so you can notice it.
- **A hissing or blowing sound** near a pipe, a gas appliance or the meter.
- **A fuel gas alarm sounds**, if you have one.
- Do not rely on your nose alone: after a minute or two of smelling the same odor, your nose adapts and you stop noticing it. If you suspect a leak, act the same even if the smell seems to fade.

## What to do, step by step
1. **No flames or sparks.** Put out cigarettes and any open flame right away. Do not light matches or lighters.
2. **Touch nothing electrical.** Do not turn switches on or off, do not use a landline or cell phone inside the house, and do not plug or unplug appliances: one spark is enough to ignite the gas.
3. **Ventilate only if it does not delay you.** If you pass a door or window on your way out, open it as you go. Do not spend time opening all of them.
4. **Leave at once, with everyone.** Take all people and pets out. Do not stop for belongings, and avoid the elevator if you can.
5. **Do not go back in** to shut off the gas or fetch anything. If the main valve is outside and you can close it without going in or delaying your exit, do so; otherwise leave it to the professionals.
6. **Call from outside.** Once well away from the house, or from a neighbor's home, call your emergency number and the gas company. Always report a suspected leak, even if it seems small.
7. **Wait outside** until the gas company or firefighters say it is safe to go back.

## Afterwards
- Do not turn the gas back on or use gas appliances again on your own: the provider must check the whole system and make sure no leak remains before they are used again.
- Bottled gas (propane or butane) follows the same rule: get out and call from outside.

## Important
