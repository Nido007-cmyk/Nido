Heat can kill. Heat stroke is an emergency: the body can no longer cool itself.

## Heat exhaustion
Signs: heavy sweating, tiredness, dizziness, headache, nausea, cramps, cool and clammy skin.
1. Move the person to shade or a cool place. Have them lie down.
2. Remove extra clothing. Cool them with water and moving air (fanning).
3. If they are awake and not vomiting, give water or a drink with salts in sips.
4. If there is no improvement in 30 minutes, or they get worse, call the emergency number.

## Heat stroke (emergency)
Signs: confusion, strange behavior, slurred speech, fainting or seizures; very hot skin (dry or sweaty); very high temperature.
1. Call the emergency number.
2. Cool them immediately, without waiting: the best method is to immerse the body (from the neck down) in cold or ice water. Keep the head above water.
3. If there is no bath or river: soak them with cold water, cover the whole body with wet sheets or towels with ice and change them often, and fan non-stop.
4. Keep cooling until they recover consciousness or help arrives.
5. If they are not awake, do not give them anything to drink.

## Prevention
- Drink water often, even if you are not thirsty. Avoid alcohol.
- Avoid sun and exercise in the hottest hours.
- Light, light-colored clothing, a hat.
- Check on babies, older people, sick people and anyone living alone. Never leave a child or an animal inside a car.
