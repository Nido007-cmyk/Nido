Wildfires move very fast, especially with wind and uphill.

## Prepare
- Clear a strip around your home of dry vegetation, firewood and rubbish.
- Keep your go-bag ready and your car fueled and facing the way out.
- Know at least two evacuation routes.
- Keep FFP2/N95 masks for smoke.

## When there is a warning
- **Leave early.** If you're told to evacuate, do it at once. Waiting until the last moment is the most dangerous choice: smoke and traffic block roads.
- Cotton or wool clothing (not synthetic), long sleeves, closed shoes.
- Close doors and windows as you leave (don't lock interior doors), turn off the gas.

## If the fire traps you
- **In a car**: park away from vegetation, in a clear area. Close windows and vents. Leave the engine and lights on. Lie on the floor of the car and cover yourself with a wool blanket or coat. Stay inside until the fire front has passed.
- **On foot**: find an area without vegetation (wide road, rock, already-burned ground, water). Lie face down with your feet toward the fire, covering yourself with soil or clothing. Don't run uphill.
- **At home**: if you can't leave, stay inside, away from outside walls, with doors and windows closed. Fill containers with water.

## Smoke
Wildfire smoke harms you even far away. Stay indoors with windows closed, use an air purifier if you have one, and an FFP2/N95 mask if you go out. Take special care of children, older people, pregnant women and people with asthma or heart problems.

## Important
Based on Ready.gov (FEMA), the American Red Cross, CAL FIRE and the WHO. Always follow evacuation orders from the authorities.
