## Before
If you live near an active volcano, know the hazard zones, evacuation routes and the alert system. Keep FFP2/N95 masks and sealed goggles.

## During
- Follow evacuation orders. Move upwind if you can.
- Avoid valleys and riverbeds coming down from the volcano: **lahars** (rivers of mud and debris) can travel very far, very fast.
- If you are close, protect yourself from falling rocks: cover your head.

## Ashfall
- Stay inside with doors and windows closed. Block gaps with damp towels.
- If you go out: FFP2/N95 mask (or, if none, a damp cloth), sealed goggles (no contact lenses), long sleeves.
- Protect water and food. Cover water tanks. If water has ash in it, don't drink it.
- Avoid driving: ash is slippery, reduces visibility and damages engines.
- Clear ash from roofs (it is very heavy, especially when wet) carefully, without climbing up if it isn't safe.
- People with asthma or breathing problems: as protected as possible.

## Volcanic gases
Gases can collect in low areas near volcanoes. If you smell rotten eggs or have trouble breathing, move to higher ground and away.
