## Go-bag (one per person, ready by the door)
Meant for 72 hours away from home.
- **Water**: about 4 liters (1 gallon) per person per day (2 for drinking and the rest for cooking and hygiene). In the bag, what you can carry plus a filter or purification tablets.
- **Food** that won't spoil and needs no cooking: bars, nuts, ring-pull cans, crackers.
- Flashlight (a headlamp is better) and spare batteries.
- Battery or hand-crank radio to hear alerts.
- Charged power bank and cables. A phone with NIDO paired with your family.
- First aid kit and your medicines for a week, spare glasses.
- Whistle to call for help.
- Masks (dust, smoke, ash).
- Foil blanket, warm clothes, raincoat, sturdy shoes.
- Knife or multitool, duct tape, rope.
- Lighter and matches in a waterproof bag.
- Hygiene: soap, hand sanitizer, toilet paper, sanitary pads, garbage bags.
- Copies of documents in a waterproof bag, cash in small bills.
- Contact list on paper and a map of the area.
- For babies: diapers, formula, bottle. For pets: food, water, leash, carrier.

## Supplies at home (for sheltering in place)
- Water for at least 3 days, better 2 weeks (4 liters per person per day).
- Canned and dry food for 2 weeks, and a manual can opener.
- Camping stove and fuel, only for use outdoors or with good ventilation.
- Flashlights, candles used with care, batteries.
- Unscented bleach to purify water and disinfect.
- Large garbage bags and buckets (for an emergency toilet).

## Upkeep
- Check every 6 months: expiry dates, batteries, clothes for the season.
- Rotate bottled water by its date. Tap water in clean containers, every 6 months.
- Also keep a small kit in the car and at work.
