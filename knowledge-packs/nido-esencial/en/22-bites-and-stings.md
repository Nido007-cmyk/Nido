## Dog, cat or other mammal bite
1. If it bleeds heavily, press hard (see severe bleeding).
2. Wash the wound thoroughly with soap and water for about 15 minutes. This is the most important step against rabies.
3. Cover with a clean dressing.
4. Get medical care: they may need antibiotics, a tetanus shot or rabies vaccine. If possible, find out whether the animal is vaccinated. Bats and wild animals always need a medical visit.

## Snakebite
1. Move away from the snake. Do not try to catch or kill it (a photo from a distance helps).
2. Call the emergency number.
3. Keep the person still and calm. Immobilize the limb as if it were a fracture, roughly at heart level.
4. Remove rings, watches and tight clothing: the area will swell.
5. Do NOT cut, do NOT suck out venom, do NOT apply a tourniquet, do NOT apply ice, do NOT use electric shocks.
6. What saves lives is antivenom in a hospital: get there as soon as possible.
Note: in Australia and some regions, for snakes whose venom affects the nerves, a pressure bandage over the whole limb is used; only if local services advise it.

## Bee, wasp and ant stings
- Remove the stinger as soon as possible. Wash, apply cold wrapped in cloth.
- Watch for signs of severe allergy (trouble breathing, swelling of the face): see the anaphylaxis guide.

## Ticks
- Remove it as soon as possible with fine tweezers, gripping as close to the skin as possible and pulling straight out, steadily and slowly. Do not crush it or put oil, alcohol or fire on it.
- Wash the area. If in the following weeks a growing red rash, fever or aches appear, see a doctor.

## Spiders and scorpions
- Wash and apply cold. If there is severe pain, sweating, cramps or trouble breathing, or if it is a young child, call the emergency number.

## Jellyfish
1. Get out of the water. Remove tentacles with tweezers or a gloved hand.
2. Don't rub the area.
3. For pain, immerse or rinse the area in water as hot as can be tolerated without scalding (about 20 minutes).
4. If beach lifeguards advise a different treatment for local jellyfish, follow it.
