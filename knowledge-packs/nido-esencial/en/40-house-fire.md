In a fire you may have only 2 minutes to get out. Smoke kills more people than flames.

## Before
- Install smoke alarms on every floor and in bedrooms. Test them every month.
- Have an escape plan: two ways out of each room and a meeting point outside.
- Keep a fire extinguisher in the kitchen and learn to use it.
- Don't leave cooking unattended. Don't overload sockets. Be careful with candles, heaters and damaged chargers.

## If there is a fire
1. Shout "Fire!" and get out at once. Don't stop to take things.
2. **Get low**: the cleanest air is near the floor. Crawl if there is smoke.
3. Before opening a door, touch it with the back of your hand. If it's hot, don't open it: use another exit.
4. Close doors behind you: they slow fire and smoke.
5. Use the stairs, never the elevator.
6. Once outside, stay outside. Call the emergency number. Never go back in.

## If you can't get out
- Close the door and block the gaps with towels or clothes (wet is better).
- Call the emergency number and say exactly where you are.
- Go to a window and signal with a flashlight or a light-colored cloth.

## If your clothes catch fire
**Stop, drop and roll**, covering your face with your hands, until the flames are out. Then cool the burn with water.

## Cooking oil fire
Turn off the heat and cover the pan with a lid or a damp (not wet) cloth. **Never throw water on it.**

## Using an extinguisher
Only if the fire is small and the exit is behind you: pull the pin, aim at the base of the flames, squeeze the lever and sweep from side to side.
