Diarrhea kills through dehydration, especially young children and older people. The solution is simple: replace water and salts.

## Oral rehydration solution
Best are oral rehydration salts (ORS) sachets from a pharmacy or health center. Prepare them as the sachet says.

**If there are no sachets, the WHO homemade recipe:**
- 1 liter of safe water (boiled and cooled, or treated).
- 6 level teaspoons of sugar.
- Half a level teaspoon of salt.
Stir until dissolved. It should taste no saltier than tears: if it does, add water. Use it within 24 hours.

## How to give it
- Give small, frequent sips, or by teaspoon for children, even if they vomit: wait 10 minutes and continue more slowly.
- As a guide, after each loose stool: half a cup (50 to 100 ml) under 2 years, a cup (100 to 200 ml) from 2 to 10 years, and as much as they want for older people.
- Keep breastfeeding babies and give normal food as soon as they are hungry. No need to fast.
- In children, zinc for 10 to 14 days shortens diarrhea (as the health center advises).
- Avoid soft drinks, commercial juices and sports drinks as a substitute: they have too much sugar.

## Signs of dehydration
Great thirst, dry mouth, little and dark urine, sunken eyes, crying without tears, skin that goes back slowly when pinched, listlessness.

## Get urgent medical care if...
- They are very weak, cannot drink, or vomit everything.
- Blood in the stool, high fever, severe stomach pain.
- Signs of dehydration that don't improve.
- It is a young baby, or an older or frail person.
- Very heavy, watery diarrhea ("rice water"), especially during an outbreak or after floods (possible cholera): keep rehydrating and get help.

## Prevention
Safe water, handwashing with soap, clean latrines, well-cooked food.
