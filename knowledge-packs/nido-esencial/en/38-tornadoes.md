A tornado is a violently rotating column of air. It can form within minutes.

## Signs
A very dark or greenish sky, large hail, a low rotating cloud, a loud roar like a freight train. Listen for official warnings.

## Where to shelter
- **In a house or building**: go to a basement or storm shelter. If there is none, an interior room on the lowest floor with no windows (bathroom, closet, hallway), as far as possible from outside walls.
- Crouch down and cover your head and neck with your arms. Put a mattress, blankets or a helmet over you if you have one.
- **Mobile home, trailer or tent**: not safe. Go to a nearby sturdy building before it arrives.
- **In a car**: if you can, drive to a sturdy building. If there is no time, stay buckled in, get down below the windows and cover your head; or, if there is an area clearly lower than the road, lie in it covering your head.
- Don't shelter under a bridge or overpass.
- Don't try to outrun a tornado in urban areas or traffic.

## After
- Beware of downed power lines, glass, nails and damaged buildings.
- Wear sturdy shoes and gloves.
- Help injured people without putting yourself at risk and call the emergency number.
