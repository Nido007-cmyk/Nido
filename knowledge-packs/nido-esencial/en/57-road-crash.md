## 1. Protect
- Park your vehicle off the road and beyond the crash, with hazard lights on.
- Put on a reflective vest before getting out.
- Mark the scene (warning triangles or the beacon your country requires) to prevent another crash.
- Turn off the engines of the crashed vehicles if you can, and don't smoke. Watch for spilled fuel, wires and other hazards.

## 2. Call
Call the emergency number: exact location (road, kilometer or mile marker, direction), number of vehicles and injured, whether people are trapped, fire, or hazardous materials.

## 3. Care
- Don't move the injured unless there is immediate danger (fire, risk of another crash): they may have spinal injuries.
- If the injured person is conscious, talk to them, reassure them and tell them not to move.
- **Don't remove a motorcyclist's helmet**, unless they are not breathing and it is essential to open the airway (and preferably with two people, keeping the head in line).
- If they don't respond and are not breathing normally, start CPR.
- Control severe bleeding by pressing (see severe bleeding).
- Keep the injured warm. Don't give food or drink.

## If your car goes into water
Undo your seatbelt, lower or break the window (at a corner) and get out at once, before it sinks. Get children out first. Don't wait for it to fill with water.

## Prevention
Always wear a seatbelt, use the right child seats, a helmet on motorbikes and bicycles, never alcohol or a phone while driving.
