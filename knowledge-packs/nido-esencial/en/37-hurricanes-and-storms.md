Hurricanes (also called cyclones or typhoons) bring very strong winds, torrential rain and floods. The deadliest part is usually the water: storm surge on the coast and flooding inland.

## Before the season
- Find out whether you are in an evacuation zone and what your route is.
- Prepare supplies for 2 weeks, medicines and your go-bag.
- Check the roof, trim nearby trees and have material to protect windows (shutters or boards).

## When it approaches (warning)
- Follow official alerts on the radio or official sources.
- If ordered to evacuate, do it. Few things are worth your life.
- Bring in or tie down anything that could blow away (garden furniture, pots).
- Fill tanks, the bathtub and containers with water.
- Charge phones and power banks. Fill the car's tank.
- Turn the fridge to its coldest setting.

## During
- Stay indoors, away from windows, in an interior room on the lowest floor that won't flood.
- **The eye of the storm**: if everything suddenly goes calm, don't go out. It is the center of the storm, and the wind will return strongly from the other side.
- If water rises, go to a higher floor (not a closed attic).

## After
- Wait for the official all-clear before going out.
- Beware of downed power lines, unstable trees, standing water and damaged buildings.
- Don't use generators indoors or in the garage (carbon monoxide).
- See the floods guide for water and food.
