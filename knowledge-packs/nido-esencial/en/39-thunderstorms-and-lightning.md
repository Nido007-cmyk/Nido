**When thunder roars, go indoors: if you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck.** There is no safe place outdoors during a thunderstorm.

## What to do
- Go into an enclosed building or a hard-topped metal vehicle with the windows up, as soon as you hear the first thunder.
- Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going out.
- Indoors: avoid water (showers, washing dishes), corded devices that are plugged in, and windows.

## If you are caught outside with no shelter
This only lowers the risk a little; it does not remove it:
- Get off high places (peaks, ridges) and out of water (pool, lake, sea).
- Stay away from isolated trees, poles, metal fences and tall objects.
- Don't stay in a group: spread out several meters apart.
- In open ground, look for the lowest area.

## If someone is struck by lightning
- The person does not carry an electric charge: it is safe to touch them.
- Call the emergency number.
- If they don't respond and are not breathing normally, start CPR at once and use a defibrillator. Lightning victims often recover with fast CPR.
- If there are several victims, help first those who appear dead (not breathing): those who move or talk usually survive.
- Afterwards, check for burns and other injuries.

## Important
