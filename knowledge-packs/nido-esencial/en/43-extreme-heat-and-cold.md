## Heat wave
- Spend the hottest hours in the coolest place: lower rooms, in the shade, or air-conditioned public places ("cooling centers").
- During the day, close blinds and windows facing the sun; ventilate at night.
- Drink water often even if you are not thirsty. Avoid alcohol and heavy meals.
- Cool showers or wet cloths. Light, light-colored clothing.
- Avoid exercise and physical work in the middle of the day.
- Call or visit older, sick or isolated people daily.
- Never leave anyone (children, older people, animals) in a closed car.
- Learn the signs of heat stroke (see that guide): it is an emergency.

## Extreme cold
- Stay indoors if you can. Heat a single room and close the others.
- Dress in layers, cover your head, hands and feet. Stay dry.
- Eat and drink something warm. Avoid alcohol: it feels warming but cools the body.
- **Carbon monoxide danger**: never use braziers, barbecues, camping stoves, generators or a gas oven to heat the home. Have a CO alarm.
- Keep electric heaters away from curtains and furniture, and never leave them on unattended while sleeping.
- If you drive, carry blankets, water, food, a flashlight and a full tank. If you get stuck in snow, stay in the car, run the engine only 10 minutes per hour with a window slightly open and the exhaust pipe clear of snow.
- Learn the signs of hypothermia and frostbite (see that guide).
