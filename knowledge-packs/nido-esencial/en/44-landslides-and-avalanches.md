## Landslides and mudflows
They are more likely after heavy rain, fires or earthquakes, on steep slopes and in channels.

### Warning signs
- New cracks in the ground, walls or roads.
- Trees, poles or fences starting to tilt.
- Water springing from the ground in new places, or a stream that suddenly turns muddy or stops flowing.
- Loud cracking sounds or rocks knocking together.

### What to do
- If there is a warning or you see signs, move out of the path: go sideways, not downhill.
- During heavy storms in a risk area, stay awake and alert.
- If you cannot escape, curl into a ball and protect your head.
- Afterwards, keep away from the area: more slides can happen. Report trapped people without going in.

## Avalanches (snowy mountains)
- Check the avalanche bulletin before going out, and carry equipment (transceiver, shovel, probe) and have training.
- If caught: try to get out to the side, grab onto something, "swim" to stay on top.
- When it stops, make an air space in front of your face and push an arm upward if you can.
- If you see someone buried: note the last point where you saw them, call the emergency number and search at once. The first 15 minutes are key.
