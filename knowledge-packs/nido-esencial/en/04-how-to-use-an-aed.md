An AED (automated external defibrillator) checks the heart and gives a shock if needed. It is made for anyone to use: it talks to you and tells you what to do. It only shocks if the heart needs it. You often find them in airports, stations, shopping centers, gyms and offices (look for a heart with a lightning bolt).

## Steps
1. Turn it on (button, or by opening the lid) and follow what it says.
2. Bare the person's chest. If it is wet, dry it. If it is very hairy, shave it if the AED has a razor.
3. Stick the pads as the picture shows: one below the right collarbone and the other on the left side, below the armpit.
4. Let it analyze: say out loud "Nobody touch the person!"
5. If it says to shock: make sure nobody is touching them and press the button (some shock on their own).
6. Go straight back to CPR. Do not remove the pads: the AED will analyze again every 2 minutes.

## Precautions
- If the person is in water, move them to a dry place. It can be used on snow or wet ground.
- If there is a pacemaker bump, place the pad a little to the side, not on top.
- Remove medicine patches from the chest (with gloves) and wipe the skin.

## Important
Based on AHA and Red Cross guidelines. While someone fetches the AED, keep doing compressions. Call your emergency number.
