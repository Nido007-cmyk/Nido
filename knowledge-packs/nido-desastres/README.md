# NIDO Desastres por región / NIDO Disasters / NIDO Desastres por região

Guías prácticas de NIDO para desastres concretos que el pack Esencial no
cubre, y para las horas y los días después: tormenta de polvo, tormenta
invernal que aísla, sequía, volver a casa, quedar atrapado y las primeras
72 horas. 6 guías en español, las mismas 6 en inglés y las mismas 6 en
portugués.

Practical NIDO guides for specific disasters the Essentials pack does not
cover, and for the hours and days afterwards: dust storms, isolating winter
storms, drought, returning home, being trapped and the first 72 hours.
6 guides in Spanish, the same 6 in English and the same 6 in Portuguese.

Guias práticos da NIDO para desastres concretos que o pacote Essencial não
cobre, e para as horas e os dias seguintes: tempestade de poeira, tempestade
de inverno que isola, seca, voltar para casa, ficar preso e as primeiras
72 horas. 6 guias em espanhol, os mesmos 6 em inglês e os mesmos 6 em
português.

## Fuentes / Sources

Cada guía cita las suyas en su cabecera (`sources:`). Las principales:
Each guide cites its own in its header (`sources:`). The main ones:

- Ready.gov (FEMA): winter weather, drought, earthquakes, emergency plan.
- US National Weather Service (NOAA): dust storms and haboobs, high wind safety, winter weather.
- American Red Cross: safety steps for returning home after the flood; Canadian Red Cross: helping after a disaster.
- CENAPRED (México): what to do after an earthquake, including if you are trapped.

Las organizaciones citadas no han escrito ni revisado estas guías y no
