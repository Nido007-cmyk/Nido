A minor bump, with nobody hurt and both cars still driveable, is mostly settled with well-made paperwork. The road traffic crash guide in NIDO Essentials covers the case with injuries; this one covers yours: safety first, evidence next, and the statement form without blocking the road.

## 1. Check that nobody is hurt
<!-- questions: we bumped into each other and nobody seems hurt, what do I do first | a collision between two cars and everyone is fine, is it still an emergency | rear end shunt at the lights, both cars dented but everyone fine -->
- Ask everyone in both cars. If anyone is injured or feels unwell, it is no longer a minor crash: call the emergency number and follow the road traffic crash guide.
- Turn on the hazard lights and mark the scene if you will be stopped on the road.

## 2. Get out of the traffic flow
<!-- questions: another car hit mine from behind at a red light, nobody got hurt | bumped from behind at a traffic light, only bodywork damage | hit from behind while stopped, just a dented bumper and a cracked light -->
- First, get to a safe place on the road to exchange details.
- Before moving them, if it is safe, take the position photos (below).

## 3. Take photos before signing anything
<!-- questions: what pictures of the damage to both cars do I need after a collision | the other car hit mine, what evidence should I gather for the insurer -->
- Number plates and models of the vehicles involved.
- The damage to each car, close up and from a distance.
- The position of the vehicles.
- The more evidence you have, the easier the insurers' work afterwards.

## 4. Exchange details
<!-- questions: after a small crash with another car, what details do we swap before leaving -->
- Name and identity document of each driver.
- Number plate, make and model of each vehicle.
- Insurance company and policy number for each of you.
- Exact place, date and time.
- If there were witnesses, ask for their contact details and write them down.

## 5. Fill in the friendly statement
<!-- questions: how do we record a collision from behind on the accident statement form -->
- Fill in the European Accident Statement or the friendly accident statement (Declaración Amistosa de Accidente).
- Include all the details of the crash and the damage to people and property, and send it to your insurance company as soon as you can.
- In Brazil, on federal highways, the no-injury equivalent is the Federal Highway Police's Traffic Accident Declaration (DAT), filled in online.

## 6. If you cannot agree
<!-- questions: the driver who hit me from behind won't give me his insurance details -->
- Do not sign a statement you disagree with: fill in the statement with your own version and collect the other driver's details.
- If the other driver will not cooperate or leaves, note the number plate, keep your photos and the witnesses' contact details.
- If anyone is injured, it is no longer a minor crash: follow the road traffic crash guide in NIDO Essentials (guide 57).

## Important
Based on Revista DGT (Spain) and the Federal Highway Police FAQ (Brazil). Forms and deadlines change by country and by policy: follow your insurer's instructions.
